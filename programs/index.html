Loan programs

Every program here is one Kia's lender partner, Jason Robinson with Prosperity Home Mortgage, works with. Income limits, price limits and terms change, so the final word always comes from a full review of the buyer's situation.

MOKSLocation-based

USDA Guaranteed

$0 down
  • Home must sit in a USDA-eligible area. Many KC suburbs and outer-ring towns qualify.
  • Household income up to 115% of the area median.
  • Primary residence only. First-time status not required.
MODown payment help

MHDC First Place

4% assistance
  • Down payment help equal to 4% of the loan, forgiven after 10 years in the home.
  • For first-time buyers and veterans, or any buyer in a targeted area.
  • 640 minimum credit score. Works with FHA, VA, USDA and conventional.
  • Income and price limits apply by county. PHM is a preferred MHDC lender.
MOKSFHADown payment help

FHA Zero Down: Forgivable

$0 down
  • Covers the 3.5% FHA down payment with a second loan at 0% interest and no monthly payment.
  • Forgiven after 5 years if the home stays your primary residence and you don't fall 90+ days behind in the first 60 payments.
  • Sell, refinance or move out before year 5 and the full amount is due.
  • 640 minimum credit score (660 for manufactured homes).
  • No first-time buyer requirement and no income limit.
MOKSFHADown payment help

FHA Zero Down: Repayable

3.5% or 5%
  • Assistance of 3.5% or 5% of the price for down payment and closing costs.
  • Repaid as a second loan with a monthly payment on a 30-year schedule. The remaining balance is due in year 10.
  • 660 minimum credit score.
  • No first-time buyer requirement and no income limit.
MOKSLocation-based

Chase Community Lending

Neighborhood pricing
  • A conventional loan program for homes in Chase's designated Community Lending neighborhoods.
  • Eligibility is based on the property's address, not the buyer's income.
  • Benefits depend on the neighborhood's tier. Ask us what applies to the home you're looking at.
MOKSConventional

HomeReady and Home Possible

3% down
  • Conventional loans with reduced mortgage insurance.
  • Household income at or below 80% of the area median.
  • First-time status not required. Can pair with down payment assistance.
MOKSMilitary

VA

$0 down
  • For eligible veterans, active-duty service members and some surviving spouses.
  • No monthly mortgage insurance.
  • Veterans can also use MHDC First Place without being first-time buyers.
MOKSGovernment

FHA

3.5% down
  • Flexible credit guidelines for buyers rebuilding or just starting out.
  • Gift funds allowed for the down payment.
  • Pairs with MHDC First Place in Missouri.
MOKSLuxury

Jumbo

Over $832,750
  • For loan amounts above the 2026 conforming limit in the KC metro.
  • Options for buyers whose wealth sits in investment accounts rather than W-2 pay.
  • Jason works closely with wealth managers and financial advisors on structure.
