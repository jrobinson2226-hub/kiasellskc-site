About Kia

From building homes to helping you find HOME

My professional career started in 2005 in homebuilding, which opened the door to my future passion for home sales. Coordinating the build process of over 500 homes at a time gave me deep, hands-on experience in the real estate world.

A key part of my heart for this work comes from my own family's experience walking through the market crash of 2008. It affected not just my job but our home, and our next steps were crucial. We moved across the country, rented for a couple of years, and eventually found not just our fixer-upper to buy but our forever hometown, Olathe, Kansas. Sometimes what feels like a tragedy can bring you such blessings.

In 2012 I moved from real estate to healthcare management. That move was pivotal, because it's where I found my natural strengths in communication, customer service and leadership. In 2017 I returned to real estate, earned my license and began serving families across the Kansas City metro.

Being a mom of three amazing, busy kids has taught me to multitask, stay organized and stay connected to the community where we've truly found our HOME. Let me be the one to help your family find yours.

Questions

Answers to what buyers and sellers ask me most

Do I need a real estate agent to buy a home in Kansas City?

You don't have to have one, but most buyers are glad they did. An agent finds homes that fit, sets up showings, prices your offer from recent local sales, negotiates inspection repairs and keeps the deal on track to closing. Before we tour homes together, we'll sign a short written buyer agreement, and I'll walk you through exactly what it says, including how I'm paid.

How much do I need for a down payment in Kansas or Missouri?

Less than most people think. VA and USDA loans can be $0 down, FHA starts at 3.5% and some conventional loans start at 3%. Missouri and Kansas buyers may also qualify for down payment assistance. You can check any address with my Program & Property Finder, and my lender partner Jason Robinson can confirm what you qualify for.

Should I buy on the Kansas side or the Missouri side?

It depends on what matters most to you. Property taxes, school districts, commute and home prices all vary from city to city on both sides of State Line. I'm licensed in Kansas and Missouri, so I can show you homes on either side and help you compare them honestly.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Ask me for a free market analysis. I'll compare your home with similar homes that recently sold nearby, walk through it with you and give you a realistic price range, plus a few simple things that could help it sell for more.

How long does it take to sell a house in the Kansas City area?

It depends on your price range, neighborhood and the time of year. Well-priced, well-prepared homes in popular areas often sell quickly, while others take longer. I'll show you current numbers for your area before we set a price, so you know what to expect.

What areas of Kansas City do you serve?

I help buyers and sellers across the Kansas City metro, including Olathe, Overland Park, Lenexa, Shawnee and the rest of Johnson County, Lee's Summit, Liberty, the Northland and Kansas City, Missouri.
